About

Software engineering student, builder, and older sister in tech.

Background

I am a 3rd-year software engineering student based in Ontario, Canada, with a focus on cloud engineering, infrastructure, and building accessible software.

As a first-generation immigrant, navigating tech and university recruiting often felt like deciphering an unwritten rulebook. There were no family mentors in the industry, and the flood of advice online felt gatekept behind buzzwords and algorithmic elitism.

Why Software Engineering Newbies?

I started Software Engineering Newbies to change that. The mission is simple: eliminate the confusion, the ego, and the gatekeeping for early-stage engineering students, career switchers, and aspiring builders.

Whether breaking down Data Structures & Algorithms into plain English, sharing honest interview preparation blueprints, or mapping out cloud fundamentals, everything published here is designed to serve as clear, practical guidance from an older sister in tech.
