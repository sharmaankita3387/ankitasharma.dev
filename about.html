Background & Roots

First-Generation Immigrant & Builder

Navigating software engineering without industry connections often feels like decoding an unwritten playbook. Between algorithmic gatekeeping and conflicting online advice, it is easy to feel overwhelmed.

I chose to channel that experience into building. My journey centers on mastering cloud infrastructure, building reliable web software, and turning complex computer science concepts into clear, digestible roadmaps.

The Mission

Why Software Engineering Newbies?

I founded Software Engineering Newbies to deliver the mentorship I wished I had from day one. Acting as an "older sister in tech," the platform provides practical, ego-free guidance across technical interviews, resume building, internships, and cloud fundamentals.

The vision is grounded in a simple truth: "No gatekeeping. No ego. Just builders helping builders."
